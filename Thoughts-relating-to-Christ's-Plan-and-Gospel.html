Marriage is a chance for us to learn something about God's love for us. We choose someone, not because they're perfect, but in spite of their imperfection, like God chooses us. If we are faithful to God and to our spouse, we will be worthy of receiving full devotion to God, and to our spouse, as we give our full devotion to God and our spouse. We will be worthy of this complete devotion, both giving it and receiving it, building each other up in God's power and from God's power, forever, being a god and being with a god, in this complete unity of love and building each other up.
