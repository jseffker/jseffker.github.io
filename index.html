Finding Happiness and Power in Christ

A compilation of thoughts, scriptures, stories, and music -- centered around finding happiness and power in Christ

By Jared Seffker

What the most important thing we can know? Here's a shot: Years ago before we were all born, God made a plan and told us all about it. His Son, Jesus Christ, was at the center of that plan. He would create the world and we'd live there. He would come to earth one day and live His life and then sacrifice Himself for us, feeling extreme pain, and then die for us. He did just that. One day, He would come back to the earth and bring everyone that followed Him to live with Him forever. That day is soon. He is real, and all-powerful. He will help us follow Him.

The Israelites never had enough food for their journey but they always had enough food for the current day. It seems like that's how God asks us to live too. He did command them to be diligent in collecting manna each day, and He asks us to be diligent too, but if you are feeling overwhelmed, consider if you're trying to collect tomorrow's bread. If so, try refocusing on Christ and doing the other things He wants you to prioritize.

A lot of life has to do with waiting. In life we wait for things like waiting to wake up, for mom to come home, to grow up and undestand things better, for school to start or end, for winter break or Christmas, to have more priviledges and be able to stay up later, a call or message from a friend, an invite to a party, an answer to prayer or testimony of Christ's gospel and church, good new or to "hear the bad news", the "one", to find out if they're the "one", to get into the hospital, to get out of it, healing, forgiveness, life, death, judgement, living with God again. Alma said that time only is measured unto men. So, what's the solution to all this waiting? Will we ever reach the end of it? Maybe it just has to do with finding peace in Christ through it all. Isaiah said "Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on Thee."

Imagine the strongest kid you know (maybe in the age range of like 3-4)... How strong are they? Compared to other kids, they might be pretty strong. What about compared to an adult? Probably not that strong, no matter what kind of kid it is. The strongest kid is the one who's parent is nearby, or who stays close to their parent. It's the same with us and God. It's not our strength that the power comes from, but God's.

It takes a lot of faith in Christ to not be afraid of death, it takes even more to not be afraid of life with pain. Try to trust God.

If you want God to teach you how to be happy, look at the members of the older generation who work in the temple. They've had a lot of the things taken away that people would say are needed to be happy, but they are some of the happiest and purposeful people you'll meet because they trust in Christ.

As humans, we like "news". Because it distracts us? Maybe. Maybe it's because as humans that endure hard lives, we want to be part of something bigger than the small, difficult problems we face personally. It's our opportunity to serve Christ.

When you're enduring hard trials you can't afford cheap entertainment but have to pursue joy in Christ. You have to try to draw near to Him.

Sometimes in life we refuse to accept the happiness God offers us because we still feel pain. We don't want to believe He's in control when life is still hard. God didn't promise us an easy life, but He did say "take my yoke up you and learn of me... and ye shall find restu to your souls."

Don't worry too much about the direction your life seems to be going except whether you're developing more love/charity toward God and His children. That's what will matter most when all is said and done. Love leads us to obey God and serve Him. True love is charity, the pure love of Christ.

Paul said that if you don't have charity, you can do nothing. When Christ appeared to the disciples after His resurrection and saw that they weren't doing the work He hoped they would be, He didn't tell them to set goals, or tell them about the consequences that could be waiting -- good or bad. He simply asked them if they loved Him. "We love Him, because He first loved us." The next time you are struggling to find motivation to do something, you could try asking whether you love God, and think of all the ways He's shown love for you. Don't do it for yourself, because frankly, there's a lot God has already given us, even if we don't do everything He wants, and there's a lot of pain we might experience if we do try to do what He wants. That's the nature of having faith in Him -- we usually don't experience the full joy until after we've chosen His way. But, we can do things in love, which, although it does help us, is ultimately selfless, because we don't really know what's in store for us, but we believe in Him. Love -- charity, the pure love of Christ, acts as a power within us that we both possess but which also "possesses" us, as opposed to enmity, which is the both power satan uses and is possessed with to rule over his followers. Choosing to be possessed of charity is not a surrender of agency but a choice of how we'll use it. God said, "There is none beside Me". Ultimately, no one can tell us whether God is right, except for God Himself, and us deciding to believe Him. But, He did give us love -- love that He both showed to us but also gives us the opportunity to try and see if it is sweet. God is love, and God's gift of love is His Son, Jesus Christ. As we follow His example, follow Christ, and seek charity, the pure love of Christ, acting in love, even when it seems hard or we're unsure, we'll have the power to do what He asks as "We love Him, becuase He first loved us."

"Let us cheerfully do all things that lie in our power; and then may we stand still, with the utmost assurance, to see the salvation of God, and for his arm to be revealed." Life can be hard, especially when there are trials that seem really hard, and that seem to be stopping us from having greater happiness or accomplishing God's will.Remember this verse and remember that God is in control.

Why am I writing all this? Because I feel like has blessed me so much in my life without me deserving it, and I want to try to give back in any way I can. I feel like these writings are full of mistakes both in how I wrote them and why I wrote them, but still I'll do my best to write them for God and not myself, and write them the way He wants them written, if He wants them written at all. Everything written here is meant to be focused on Christ in some way. He's the One who can actually help people. Because of this, I'm gonna do my best not to sensationalize words or bend my writing toward certain patterns, and I'll try to be plain and concise too. I'm just gonna try to write the things I feel are true about Christ and that will help people. There will still be many mistakes.
