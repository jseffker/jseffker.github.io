Why?

My goal in writing this is to help people see Christ in their pain and find comfort through Him. Specifically,it's to help people understand pain, why they have pain, and how to find joy in Christ. I won't claim to know all the answers, but, anything that I feel like God has taught me (Who does know all the answers), and that helps people, I'll hope to put on the main page. Any other thoughts I have, whether about spiritual things, or non-spiritual, I'll probably include somewhere else. Thanks for reading this website!

Everything on the main page is meant to be written plainly, non-cleverly, non-sensationalized, non-critically-of-others, and should have a meaning that is truly helpful to others. I do have some writing in these styles mentioned, and if you'd like to read them, I'll include them elsewhere.

These thoughts are honestly written to myself at a previous time and currently. I know what I needed, and I know what I didn't need. What I needed was to hear about Christ, and what I didn't need was to see impressive words that detract from God's power by sprinkling self-glorification. So, even though that can be hard, that is my aim to not do (not self-glorify).

If the thought or idea is about Christ, or if it's about finding joy or enduring trial through Christ, it's worth being on the main page. ---------------------------------- Tone for this page: There's a lot of things I want to avoid -- being too worried, too serious, too strict, too bold, too shy, too contentious, too proud, too smart, too wordy -- the list goes on. One way to look at it though is that I want to write it as if I were talking to someone I hardly knew, that had no reason to respect me or to think there was anything I could offer them, and yet was kind. That somehow had found God in the world, even when they were never taught anything about Him, or who had endured hard trials and still was cheerful. Someone who truly didn't need anything else but Jesus -- they didn't need any fancy words or anything.
